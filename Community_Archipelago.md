# Delft (CS) Community Archipelago

Hi, thanks for your interest in the Delft Community Archipelago!

## So, what is this?
Delft Community Archipelago is an event where we play one big archipelago session with a group of people at the same time.
The first event is planned to take place around the 10th-20th of December.
The event is fully online, though you are welcome to LAN-party with other participants.
The whole event, including setup on the day itself, will take 6~8 hours depending on technical difficulties and how much we get stuck.

## What is this Archipelago thing?
As described on the Archipelago website:

> Archipelago is a cross-game modification system which randomizes different games, then uses the result to build a single unified multi-player game. Items from one game may be present in another, and you will need your fellow players to find items you need in their games to help you complete your own.

**tldr**; everyone gets to play the games they enjoy, whilst still playing together with others.

## Sounds cool, how do I join?
Through a few steps:

> [!TIP]
> Most of the steps we are about to explain can also be found in this YouTube video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkv_xOXLshw).
> The only exception is uploading in step 4, which is not covered in the video.

### Step 1: Choose a game
Any game on this list (https://lobby.finder.giving/worlds) will do.

### Step 2: Set the options
After you have selected a game, you will need to set the options for your game. To view a list of the options, visit (https://lobby.finder.giving/options) and select your game.

Most defaults will be sensible, but feel free to have a look in the linked channels when clicking on the game at (https://lobby.finder.giving/worlds) for any tips. 
_Note that these channels will be general support for these games, and they are not associated or related to the Delft Community Archipelago_.

### Step 3: Test your settings
We ask you to aim for your individual game(s) to take about *3 hours* on their own for people who have never played Archipelago before. For those who have played before, we ask you to limit it to 4 hours.

The best way to determine if this is true for your game(s) and settings is to play a solo run.
I recommend you watch this video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kkv_xOXLshw) to find out how to set up a solo game.

### Step 4: Submit your settings
After you have found settings that work, you can submit your settings file (`.yaml`) to the room (_a link to the room will be inserted here at a later point_).
Once you have successfully uploaded your settings files to the room, your name should now show in the table. Congrats, you are ready for the event!

After the submission deadline has passed, more information will be shared to help you set up for the archipelago.
